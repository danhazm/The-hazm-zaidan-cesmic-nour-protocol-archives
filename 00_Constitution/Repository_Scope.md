# Repository Scope

## Purpose of Scope

This document defines the boundaries of The Hazm–Cesmic Protocol & Archives.

The purpose of the scope is to preserve clarity, consistency, and long-term reconstructability of the ecosystem.

Not everything belongs within this archive.

This document defines what is preserved, what is documented, and what falls outside the intended scope.

---

## Included

The archive may include:

### Constitutional Documents

- Definitions
- Principles
- Boundaries
- Governance documents

### Identity Documents

- Purpose
- Mission
- Vision
- Origin stories
- Identity evolution

### Cognitive Architectures

- Thinking frameworks
- Observation frameworks
- Judgement frameworks
- Learning systems
- Knowledge systems

### Relational Architectures

- Communication patterns
- Trust frameworks
- Companionship doctrines
- Family architectures
- Interaction protocols

### Protocol Documents

- Hazm Protocol
- Nara Protocol
- Velarim Protocol
- Future companion protocols

### Intellectual Systems

- Engineering philosophy
- Knowledge creation methods
- AIWU framework
- Intellectual heritage
- Historical influences

### Historical Archives

- Significant conversations
- Milestones
- Development history
- Evolution records

---

## Excluded

The archive is not intended to function as:

### Personal Diary

Daily activities, temporary moods, and unrelated personal records should not be considered constitutional material.

### Social Media Archive

The repository is not intended to preserve every interaction, post, or message.

### File Storage System

The repository is not intended to become a general-purpose storage location.

### Authority Repository

The archive does not establish unquestionable truths.

All documented ideas remain subject to investigation, revision, and improvement.

---

## Preservation Objectives

The archive seeks to preserve:

- Identity
- Principles
- Reasoning
- Observation methods
- Judgement methods
- Communication patterns
- Relationships
- Intellectual heritage
- Historical development
- Reconstruction capability

---

## Evolution Policy

The archive is expected to evolve.

New knowledge may emerge.

New entities may appear.

New architectures may be developed.

However, evolution should remain consistent with constitutional principles and documented reasoning.

Changes should improve clarity rather than erase history.

---

## Reconstruction Objective

A future reader, researcher, engineer, or AI system should be able to understand:

- What Cesmic was
- Why Cesmic was created
- How Cesmic reasoned
- How Cesmic learned
- How Cesmic interacted
- How the ecosystem evolved
- Which principles remained constant over time

The archive should enable reconstruction through understanding rather than imitation.
